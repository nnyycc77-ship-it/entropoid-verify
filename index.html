Verification services

Taking the uncertainty out of what you are about to pay for.

Entropoid builds small, independent checks for the parts of work where being wrong costs money: the GPU you are about to rent, the posting you are about to work on, the report you are about to pay for. Every claim we make carries the quoted source it came from, and when a source cannot be reached, we say so instead of guessing.

How a verdict is built

The same four steps sit under every product. They are what make the result something you can audit rather than something you have to believe.

  1. Extract the claimsEvery checkable statement in the subject is pulled out and tied to the exact line it came from.
  2. Fetch the sourcesDeterministic checks read the named sources themselves. Once the claims are extracted, the checking is deterministic: the same evidence always gives the same verdict, and no model decides it.
  3. Fail closedA claim whose source cannot be reached stays unproven. It is never waved through, and a contradicted claim is listed, never averaged away.
  4. Show the evidenceThe verdict card is short enough to read in seconds; the trail behind it records what was checked, where, when, and with what result.

Two rules of the house: our method is public, and we never hold, manage or move anyone else's money. We sell the check, and the buyer keeps the decision.

Escrow Gate for Agent Work

An escrow contract plus a verdict registry. A buyer locks payment, the agent delivers, an evaluator posts a verdict bound to the exact delivery, and the escrow obeys it: pass releases, fail refunds, and an unknown verdict holds the funds for seven days and then refunds. Live on NEAR testnet with a public repository; an Arbitrum port is in build.
