Proof library

Anyone can claim a method. This page shows ours running.

The checks we repeat against official sources, what each one reads, and what happens when a source cannot be read. When a source refuses or times out, the result says so plainly. It is never recorded as a pass and never as a clean no-match.

  1. Sanctions change checks

    Every day we diff the full official OFAC sanctions list against the previous snapshot and record every change, with the source files and their hashes kept for each check.

  2. Public record checks

    Registry and records verification across product recalls, motor carrier records, court dockets and filing data. Each sample records the source's answer as it came back, including the ones that came back "not checked".

    Tools and sample reports

  3. Escrow dispute simulations

    Synthetic disputes run through the escrow verdict logic in bulk, covering pass, fail and the unknown case that holds funds. The simulations are how the seven-day unknown hold in Escrow Gate was shaped and tested.

    Escrow Gate repository

  4. Live delivery checks

    Our evaluator has run end to end on live paid jobs: contract in, delivery checked against sources, verdict recorded, payment released. The acceptance record is kept job by job, and the delivered reports are public.

    Delivered reports

  5. CPSC recalls dataset

    A structured dataset of United States product recalls, 9,487 records across 37 years, built from the official source, for agents and researchers who need recall history they can cite.

    Dataset
